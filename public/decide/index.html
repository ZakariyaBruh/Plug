                                          Finding what’s on…

                                            The channel list would not load.

                                            Channels from iptv-org, the open database of free TV, read fresh every hour — and each one is tried from your own browser before it is listed, so you only see the channels that actually play where you are. Each group is picked by hand from iptv-org’s own categories, with nothing grim for the dinner table. What’s on comes from each channel’s published listings, found through iptv-org/epg; a channel can run a few minutes off them. They are free, ad-supported channels — the adverts are theirs, not ours.

                                            Tuning in…

                                              The radio directory is not answering just now.

                                              It is a free, volunteer-run directory and it has the odd bad minute. Nothing is wrong at your end.

                                              Stations come from Radio Browser, a free community directory. The food and dining group is stations named for eating — dinner lounges, restaurant and coffee-bar channels, a barbecue station; the others are music to cook and eat to, about ten of the best-loved stations in each, with no talk, news or sport. Each streams straight from its own broadcaster to you; morsels45 hosts none of them and puts nothing over them, though a station may run its own adverts.

                                                                              The shared browser

                                                                              A real web browser, running in the cloud, that two of you use at the same time — each from your own phone, each with your own cursor. You open it here, send the other person a link, and whatever either of you does, both of you see. No screen-sharing, no passing one phone round the table.

                                                                              Where you’ll find it

                                                                              1. 🛵 Order it together — on the answer screen, once you accept a dish. Opens a delivery site on that dish so you can both pick and pay in one basket.
                                                                              2. 📍 Order from here — in Nearby, on any restaurant. Opens that place’s own ordering page.
                                                                              3. 🧑‍🍳 Cook along — in cook mode, on any recipe. One cooking video, in step, in two kitchens.
                                                                              4. 🛒 Shop it together — on any shopping list: a recipe’s, or your whole week’s in the Planner. One supermarket basket, filled by both of you.

                                                                              How to use it

                                                                              1. Tap one of those buttons. The browser opens here in a few seconds.
                                                                              2. Tap Copy link and send it to whoever you’re with.
                                                                              3. They open it on their phone or laptop — nothing to install — and you each get a cursor.

                                                                              It is part of Premium because it is a real computer running for you while it is open: the one part of morsels45 that costs real money by the minute.
